Honest limits

  • Closed 20-question set: 20/20 is on questions the parser supports, not open-ended QA.
  • The 0.5B LLM mis-routes some questions; its composed answers are natural language over the graph evidence, never computed values.
  • Both baselines are deliberately simple (top-50 retrieval); they retrieve chunks and take the best value they find, they do not aggregate over the whole dataset.
